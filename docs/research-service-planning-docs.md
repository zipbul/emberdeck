# Research: Service Planning Documents — Full Scope

Date: 2026-04-06

## Summary
Research on planning documents for services across all domains — not limited to software development. Covers service design, business modeling, UX planning, policy/operations, startup planning, academic frameworks, and government standards. All claims are source-backed with URLs.

---

## 1. Service Design

### 1-1. Service Blueprint (G. Lynn Shostack, 1984, HBR)
5 lanes: Physical Evidence, Customer Actions, Frontstage Actions, Backstage Actions, Support Processes
- https://servicedesigntools.org/tools/service-blueprint
- https://www.nngroup.com/articles/service-blueprinting-template/
- https://www.lucidchart.com/blog/what-is-a-service-blueprint

### 1-2. Double Diamond (Design Council, 2004)
4 phases: Discover → Define → Develop → Deliver (divergent-convergent-divergent-convergent)
- https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/our-resources/the-double-diamond/
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_Diamond_(design_process_model)

### 1-3. IDEO Human-Centered Design (2009/2015)
3 phases: Inspiration → Ideation → Implementation. 150,000+ downloads, open-source methodology.
- https://www.ideo.com/journal/design-kit-the-human-centered-design-toolkit
- https://www.designkit.org/

### 1-4. IBM Enterprise Design Thinking
3 principles: User outcomes, Restless reinvention, Diverse empowered teams
3 practices: Hills, Playbacks, Sponsor Users
The Loop: Observe → Reflect → Make
- https://www.ibm.com/design/approach/design-thinking/
- https://www.ibm.com/design/thinking/page/framework

### 1-5. UK GDS Service Standard (14 criteria, 2019)
End-to-end service design standard for government services.
- https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard

## 2. Business Modeling

### 2-1. Business Model Canvas (Osterwalder & Pigneur, 2005)
9 blocks: Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key Activities, Key Partnerships, Cost Structure
- https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-business-model-canvas
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_model_canvas

### 2-2. Lean Canvas (Ash Maurya, 2010)
BMC adapted for startups. Replaces Key Partners/Activities/Resources/Customer Relationships with Problem, Solution, Key Metrics, Unfair Advantage.
- https://www.leanfoundry.com/tools/lean-canvas
- https://medium.com/lean-stack/what-is-the-right-fill-order-for-a-lean-canvas-f8071d0c6c8c

### 2-3. Value Proposition Canvas (Osterwalder)
Deep-dive into BMC's value proposition block. Customer Profile (Jobs, Pains, Gains) ↔ Value Map (Products/Services, Pain Relievers, Gain Creators)
- https://www.strategyzer.com/library/mastering-value-propositions

### 2-4. BRD vs PRD
- BRD (Business Requirements Document): What & Why, strategic, by Business Analyst
- PRD (Product Requirements Document): How, detailed features/UX, by Product Manager
- BRD comes first → PRD refines
- https://asana.com/resources/business-requirements-document-template
- https://www.atlassian.com/agile/product-management/requirements

### 2-5. Amazon Working Backwards PR/FAQ
PR structure: Heading, Summary, Problem, Solution, Quotes, CTA
FAQ structure: External FAQ (pricing, operations) + Internal FAQ (market size, risks, competition)
- https://workingbackwards.com/concepts/working-backwards-pr-faq-process/
- https://workingbackwards.com/resources/working-backwards-pr-faq/

## 3. UX/Product Design Planning

### 3-1. Design Brief
Contents: Brand overview, Project scope, Goals & objectives, Target audience, Budget & timeline, Competitor analysis, Deliverables
- https://asana.com/resources/design-brief
- https://www.uxpin.com/studio/blog/design-brief/

### 3-2. UX Research Plan
Contents: Background, Research objectives, Participants/recruitment, Methods, Protocol, Timeline, Budget
- https://miro.com/templates/ux-research-plan/
- https://www.eleken.co/blog-posts/ux-research-plan-template

Components: Stages, Actions, Emotions, Touchpoints, Pain Points, Opportunities
- https://www.thisisservicedesigndoing.com/methods/mapping-journeys
- https://servicedesigntools.org/tools/journey-map

### 3-4. Jobs to Be Done (Ulwick 1990, Christensen popularized)
Core idea: people "hire" products to do a "job" in their lives. Functional, social, emotional dimensions.
- https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/jobs-to-be-done/
- https://strategyn.com/jobs-to-be-done/

5-day process: Understand → Sketch → Decide → Prototype → Validate
- https://www.gv.com/sprint/
- https://designsprintkit.withgoogle.com/methodology

## 6. Korean IT Service Planning (서비스 기획)

### 6-1. Standard Structure
1. 서비스 개요 — 목표, 주요 기능
2. 서비스 구성 — IA, 서비스 정책, 주요 프로세스
3. 상세 기획 — 화면 시나리오, 와이어프레임 + 기능 상세

Framework: Why → What → How → So What (3W 1H)

PRD contents: 개요, 문제 정의, 목적/배경, 주요 사용자, 유저 스토리/저니맵, 사용자 가치, 가설/검증 지표, 임팩트 예측

- https://brunch.co.kr/@ecobyun/6
- https://www.mobiinside.co.kr/2022/03/31/service-plan/

## 8. Academic/Industry Frameworks

### 8-1. NSD Model (Scheuing & Johnson, 1989)
15-stage model from survey of 66 financial services firms.
Simplified by Johnson et al. (2000) to 4-stage cycle: Design → Analysis → Development → Full Launch
- https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/A-Proposed-Model-for-New-Service-Development-Scheuing-Johnson/d1fb2677977d8676e821b661adc9d56b55a3da1d
- https://www.researchgate.net/publication/235262127

### 8-2. Service-Dominant Logic (Vargo & Lusch, 2004)
All exchange is service-for-service. Value is cocreated by multiple actors, not delivered by firms.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service-dominant_logic
- https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/pdf/10.1287/serv.1.1.32

### 8-3. Service Innovation Typology (Toivonen & Tuominen, 2009)
3 analysis dimensions: target of renewal, nature of renewal, radicalness of renewal
- https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1032116

### 8-4. ITIL Service Design
11 processes including Service Catalogue, Service Level, Risk, Capacity, Availability, Continuity, Security, Compliance, Architecture, Supplier management.
4P framework: People, Processes, Products, Partners
ITIL 4: shifted from prescriptive processes to 34 flexible "practices"
- https://wiki.en.it-processmaps.com/index.php/ITIL_Service_Design
- https://www.axelos.com/resource-hub/practice/service-design-itil-4-practice-guide
